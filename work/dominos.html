Dominos is a cat condo proposed to be made on Pratt Institute’s lawn for Pratt cats that live on the campus. This is a sculptural piece that can be inhabited by both cats and people. The structures are angled to replicate dominos pieces in motion. There are benches made on either sides of these structures for people to occupy while the cats get to engage with the voids made through the tilting structures.
